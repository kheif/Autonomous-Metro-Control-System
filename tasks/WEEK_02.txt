================================================================
 AMCS — WEEK 2 TASKS
 Phase 1: Simulation Core (Week 2/4)
 Goal: Many trains on one line. Detect collisions. Keep it
       deterministic. Meet the borrow checker for real.
================================================================

Each task = a goal + concepts to learn. No solutions given.
Research the "how" yourself. Struggle is the point.

Week 1 gave you ONE train that moves. Week 2 is the first time
an engineering problem forces a new component: put two trains on
the same track and the question "are they about to hit?" appears
on its own. Build only what that question needs.


----------------------------------------------------------------
## A — MANY TRAINS
----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Hold N trains in the world instead of one local variable.
[ ] Advance every train one tick, each step, in the loop.
[ ] Give each train an identity so logs/tests can name it.
[ ] Print all trains' state each tick, readably.

Learn: Vec<T>, iterating (for, iter, iter_mut), what &mut over a
collection lets you do. Why a HashMap here would quietly break
determinism (iteration order) unless you sort.
Focus: keep the update order fixed and explicit. Same order,
every run.


----------------------------------------------------------------
## B — DISTANCE & SEPARATION
----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Put all trains on one shared 1D line so "distance between
    two trains" is a meaningful number.
[ ] Compute the gap between any two trains (and to the one ahead).
[ ] Decide what "too close" means: a minimum safe separation.
    Write down WHY you picked that number.

Learn: modelling a shared coordinate space. The difference
between position, gap, and "safe following distance". Why safe
separation isn't constant in real life (hint: speed & braking) —
you don't have to solve that yet, just notice it.


----------------------------------------------------------------
## C — COLLISION DETECTION & RESPONSE
----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Write a check that scans the world and reports every pair of
    trains closer than the safe separation (or overlapping).
[ ] THE OWNERSHIP WALL: to compare train A against train B you
    need to look at two elements of the same Vec at once. The
    borrow checker will stop you. Understand exactly why, then
    get past it the RIGHT way.
[ ] Give the system one honest reaction: when a train's path is
    unsafe, put it into Emergency. Detection first, reaction second.
[ ] Prove it: build a scenario that WOULD collide (fast train
    closing on a stopped one). Show the check fires before impact.

Learn: why you cannot hold two &mut into one Vec. The legit ways
out — indices, split_at_mut, or reading positions into a snapshot
first — and the trade-offs of each.
Focus: this task IS the week. Do NOT escape with Rc/RefCell or by
cloning everything. Learn the borrow checker, don't bribe it.


----------------------------------------------------------------
## D — HABITS
----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Formatter + linter clean before every commit.
[ ] A test that two trains on a collision course are FLAGGED.
[ ] A test that two safely-separated trains are NOT flagged.
[ ] A test proving multi-train runs are still deterministic
    (two runs -> byte-identical output).
[ ] DEVLOG.md — daily: learned / broke / next.
[ ] Small daily commits.


----------------------------------------------------------------
## DONE CRITERIA
----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] World holds N trains; all advance each tick in fixed order.
[ ] Gap between trains is computable; safe separation is defined
    and justified in writing.
[ ] Collision check finds every unsafe pair; you can explain how
    you solved the two-&mut problem in your own words.
[ ] Unsafe train transitions to Emergency.
[ ] Collision + no-collision + determinism tests pass, linter clean.
[ ] Daily commits + DEVLOG on GitHub.

All checked -> Week 3 (a real track layout: blocks & signals).


----------------------------------------------------------------
## DON'T
----------------------------------------------------------------
- No signals, switches, blocks, routes or pathfinding yet.
  That's Phase 2. This week is bare positions on one line.
- No async, traits, generics, networking, DB, React.
- No "generic engine" / ECS abstraction. Just a Vec of trains.
- No HashMap for the train list (nondeterministic order) unless
  you deliberately sort and can say why.
- Don't reach for Rc<RefCell<>> or clone-everything to dodge the
  borrow checker. That's the lesson, not the obstacle.
- Don't build a full "safety layer" yet — one reaction is enough.
